# Peekaboo – all 365 daily dares 🎯

One per day, the same for everyone, in this order. Day 1 is whichever one is on today; the list starts again after a year.

House rules: nobody gets hurt · no fire · clothes stay on · friends must be in on it. Full rules: [Dare Safety Rules](../legal/DARE_SAFETY_RULES.md). What changed in the 31 October 2026 safety review: [legal/DARE_REVIEW_2026-10-31.md](../legal/DARE_REVIEW_2026-10-31.md).
