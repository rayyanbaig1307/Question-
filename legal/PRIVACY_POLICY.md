# Peekaboo – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 31 October 2026
**Who is responsible for your data ("data controller"):** [OPERATOR NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS], [CONTACT EMAIL]

This policy explains what Peekaboo collects, why, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It is written to match what the app really does. If we change how the app works, we update this policy and tell you in the app.

## 1. The short version

- We collect what you give us (account, profile, photos, videos, messages, location if you turn it on) so the app can work.
- We do **not** sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers.
- Your content is shown only to the people you choose (friends, close friends, a group), except Guess Who posts and Discover, which the app explains when you post.
- You can delete your account in Settings at any time; that deletes your data.
- Data is stored on Supabase servers in the **United States (AWS, us-east-1)**.

## 3. Who can see what (inside the app)

- **Posts, stories, drops, dares:** your friends (or only close friends, or one group, when you choose that). Discover shows posts from friends first, then from everyone who posted publicly.
- **Guess Who posts:** shown to friends without your name until they guess.
- **Peeks and messages:** only the person you sent them to.
- **Profile:** your username, display name, photo, bio, song and friend count are visible to other users who find you.
- **Location:** friends only, only while sharing is on.
- **Hit ups:** the person you hit up sees your profile and your message. If they ignore it, you are not told. Your birthday is never shown to anyone.
- **Who Peeked:** the owner of a drop can see which friends revealed it or scrolled past.

## 6. How long we keep data

| Data | Kept until |
| --- | --- |
| Stories | Deleted automatically 24 hours after posting. |
| Posts, peeks, messages, voice notes, comments, likes | Until you delete them, or your account. |
| Location | Replaced each time it updates; deleted when you turn on ghost mode or stop sharing; deleted with your account. |
| Reports and blocks | For as long as needed to keep people safe, then deleted with the account. |
| Birthday and hit ups | Kept while your account exists (so the same-age-group rule and the daily limit keep working). |
| Server logs | Up to 90 days. |
| Everything else | Deleted when you delete your account (see below). |

**Deleting your account** (Settings → Delete account) permanently deletes your profile, posts, stories, chats, friends, likes, comments, hit ups and all your files right away. Copies that other users already received (for example a saved peek or a screenshot), and backups, which are overwritten in the normal course of business within 30 days, are the only things that can outlive the deletion.

## 7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can: see the data we hold about you; correct it (most of it from Edit profile); delete it (Delete account, or email us); withdraw consent for location and notifications (phone settings / in-app switches); and ask us questions. Email [CONTACT EMAIL]; we answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm it is your account.

- **Canada (PIPEDA and provincial laws):** you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) if you are not satisfied with our answer.
- **European Economic Area / United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR):** you also have the rights to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Our legal bases are listed in section 2.
- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** you have the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing – we do not sell or share personal information. We do not discriminate against you for using your rights.
- **Other places:** the rights above are available to you too; contact us.

## 8. Children

Peekaboo is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it. Users aged 13–17 need a parent's or guardian's permission; Hit ups only connect people under 18 with other people under 18, and adults with adults.

## 9. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored on providers with industry-standard protections; passwords are hashed; database rules make sure each person can only read what they are allowed to see; access to the production database is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If we learn of a breach that puts you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 10. Screenshots and disappearing content

Stories expire and peeks are meant to be viewed once, but the people you send content to can screenshot or record it. We try to notify you when a chat or peek is screenshotted; this is best-effort and can be missed. Please post accordingly.
